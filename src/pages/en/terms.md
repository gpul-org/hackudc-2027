# Terms and conditions

## 1. Introduction

These Terms and Conditions ("T&C") govern participation in HackUDC (the "Event"), organized by the **Asociación Universitaria Grupo de Programadores y Usuarios de Linux (GPUL)** (the "Organizer").

By registering for and participating in the Event, participants accept these T&C and any additional policies linked to the Event’s challenges, evaluations, and prizes.

## 2. Definitions

- **Organizer:** Asociación Universitaria GPUL, based at Facultade de Informática, Campus de Elviña S/N, 15071 A Coruña, NIF G15659220, hackudc@gpul.org, and +34 981 167 000 ext. 1369.
- **Participant:** any person who accepts these T&C, registers for, and takes part in the Event to compete and develop projects in an established modality.
- **Mentor:** any person who accepts these T&C, registers for, and takes part in the Event as an advisor and facilitator supporting Participants and the Organizer.
- **Sponsor:** a collaborating entity which, according to its sponsorship tier, may propose challenges and/or grant specific prizes.
- **Challenges:** technical projects or tasks established by the Organizer and/or Sponsors, to be developed exclusively during the Event.
- **General Prize:** an Organizer prize for the best free-software project, based on the criteria detailed in <a href="http://premio.gpul.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Premio GPUL</a>.
- **DevPost:** the project-submission and management platform; its link will be provided during the Event.

## 3. Purpose of the Event

The Event promotes free-software development, creativity, and innovation among students and professionals. Participants will work on technical projects within the proposed challenges.

## 4. Participation rules

1. **Teams.** Teams may have up to four members.
2. **Eligibility.** Participants must be of legal age or have consent from a legal guardian.
3. **Project development.** Projects must be developed entirely during the official Event period. Participants may use libraries, frameworks, or open-source code when they comply with the applicable licences. All submissions must use a free-software licence.
4. **Submission.** Projects must be submitted through DevPost using the link supplied by the Organizer. Each team is responsible for meeting the established requirements to qualify for prizes.

## 5. Evaluation and prizes

All projects submitted through DevPost will be evaluated by a jury appointed by the Organizer, which will award the General Prize to the best free-software project.

Each Sponsor will have an independent jury and will evaluate projects against its own criteria. To compete for a sponsor prize, a team must present its project to that sponsor’s panel. Specific prizes and criteria will be announced at the opening ceremony and published on the official website.

A project that satisfies two sponsor challenges presents to the general jury and each relevant sponsor panel. It is eligible for the General Prize and all sponsor prizes whose requirements it has met.
