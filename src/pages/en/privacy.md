# Privacy policy

## Who we are

Asociación Universitaria Grupo de Programadores y Usuarios de Linux (**GPUL**), tax ID G15659220, Facultade de Informática, Campus de Elviña S/N, 15071 A Coruña, is the data controller. You can write to us at that address or at [hackudc@gpul.org](mailto:hackudc@gpul.org).

This is one policy for HackUDC and for the official hackOS instance we run. hackOS instances run by other people or organizations are independent. They need their own privacy policy, and we neither control nor process their data.

## Your data

### What we collect

**To register and take part in HackUDC,** we may process your name, contact details, date of birth, city, school or university, proof of student status, graduation year, T-shirt size, CV and any professional profiles the call asks for. We also keep records of teams, projects, challenges, evaluations and attendance. For sponsors and collaborators, we process company, role and professional contact details.

**When you use hackOS,** we process your account details, email, protected credentials, language, profile picture, the forms and files you submit, access and accreditation codes, sessions, IP address, browser and activity logs needed for security and operation, support messages, and notification preferences.

Some things are always optional and asked for separately:

- Allergies, intolerances and other dietary needs, with your explicit consent, so we can adapt the catering.
- Sharing your CV or application with sponsors, and receiving optional communications.

Most of this comes from you, or from members of your team or organization who are authorised to act for it, or is generated as you take part or use the platform. Forms mark which fields are required. Without the data a process needs, we can't complete it.

### Cookies and local storage

hackOS uses its own session cookie, which is essential to log you in and protect your account. It also stores preferences in your browser, such as language, theme, navigation state and whether you have dismissed the cookie notice. We don't use any of this for behavioural advertising or to sell information about your browsing.

### Why we use your data

- **Managing your account, application and participation:** to carry out the relationship that starts when you register and accept the terms.
- **Running the event:** accreditation, schedules, teams, projects, assessment, logistics and communications. Depending on the case, this is part of that same relationship or our legitimate interest in organizing the event.
- **Dietary information:** your explicit consent. You can withdraw it, though we may then be unable to adapt the catering to you.
- **Security and legal obligations:** our legitimate interest in keeping hackOS secure, and the laws we must comply with.
- **Telling you about similar activities:** your consent where the law requires it, or our legitimate interest where it allows. You can object at any time.
- **Identifiable photos and videos:** By attending the event in person, you declare that you understand and consent to the organisers, sponsor representatives, other participants and any other participating entity taking photos and videos in which you may appear and using them for promotional purposes, on social media, for commercial purposes or for any other purpose.
- **Working with sponsors and collaborators:** the contract or collaboration we have with them, or our legitimate interest in the business contacts it needs.
- **Sharing your CV or application with sponsors for career opportunities:** only with your specific, voluntary permission.

Accepting the terms doesn't mean consenting to everything. You can refuse optional permissions and still take part, and withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything done before you withdrew it.

## Sharing and transfers

### Who we share it with

We control authentication, databases and S3-compatible storage. They run on private infrastructure assigned to us and under our logical control, even if we don't operate the physical hardware ourselves. The servers holding accounts, applications, databases and files are in the European Economic Area.

We only share what is necessary, and only with:

- public authorities, courts and law-enforcement bodies, where the law requires it;
- **Expo,** if you enable push notifications. We send Expo Push Service your device's push token, the notification title and text, and the technical data needed to route it within the app. Expo acts as a processor delivering the message to your device's notification service;
- sponsors or assessors, where needed for a process you are part of, or where you have expressly authorised us to share your application or CV;
- advisers and auditors, where needed to meet legal obligations;
- providers who need access to deliver services we have contracted, bound by the necessary processing and confidentiality agreements;
- UDC, if you ask for participation accreditation for ECTS credits, with the identity and attendance data that process needs.

We never sell your personal data.

Push notifications may involve processing outside the European Economic Area, by Expo and by your device's operating system, of the data needed to deliver them. Where necessary, we apply the safeguards the law requires, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. Your core hackOS information stays in the EEA and is never sent to Expo.

## Keeping and protecting it

### How long we keep it, and closing your account

We keep your data while your account or relationship with GPUL is active, and afterwards only for as long as needed to deal with possible legal liabilities. Then we delete or anonymise it.

You can start closing your account from the account settings (“Danger zone”). The server checks whether you have been accredited at the event. If you haven't, the account is fully deleted where possible. If you have, it is irreversibly anonymised instead.

If you ask for this while you are recorded as inside the venue, the request is held as a pending exit. Participation services stop, and your identity and dietary data are kept only for the short time staff need to record your exit, or until the recovery deadline expires. You can cancel before then.

Anonymisation then revokes your access and removes direct identifiers, credentials, tickets, wallet authentication data, files and dietary information. It creates a new random anonymous participant record, with no lookup table or other way back to you. Only two things can remain: application answers that an authorised form administrator explicitly marked for anonymous audit in the form version you used, and verified venue-presence time generated by event operations. Everything else is removed. In a very small group, combinations of anonymous data could still point to someone, so we aggregate or suppress unusual combinations when publishing statistics. Once anonymised, we can't issue named proof of participation or ECTS documentation.

Anything we are legally required to keep is held only for the legally applicable periods. Closing your account doesn't remove those obligations.

### External links

Some forms or project records may ask for links to outside websites, such as repositories, demos or portfolios. Those sites are independent of hackOS and GPUL, aren't operated by or affiliated with us, and have their own privacy policies and terms, which apply when you visit them or send them data.

### Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your data, object to or restrict how we use it, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Email [hackudc@gpul.org](mailto:hackudc@gpul.org) with your request. We may ask for reasonable information to confirm who you are.

If you think we are breaking the law, you can complain to us or to the [Spanish Data Protection Agency](https://www.aepd.es/).

### Security, changes and contact

We use technical and organisational measures proportionate to the risk to protect data from loss, alteration, and unauthorised access or disclosure. No system is perfect, so if you spot a security problem, please tell us quickly at [hackudc@gpul.org](mailto:hackudc@gpul.org).

We may update this policy as the law or our setup changes. The current version is always on this page, and we will announce significant changes through other channels where appropriate.

For any privacy question, email [hackudc@gpul.org](mailto:hackudc@gpul.org).
