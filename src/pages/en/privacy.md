# Privacy policy

In accordance with current personal-data protection regulations, we provide the following information.

## Data Controller

The _Asociación Grupo de Programadores y Usuarios de Linux_ ("GPUL"), NIF G15659220, registered at _Facultad de Informática, Campus de Elviña S/N, 15007, A Coruña_, is the Data Controller for your personal data. For questions, requests, or clarifications, email [hackudc@gpul.org](mailto:hackudc@gpul.org).

## Data we process and how we obtain it

We process data you voluntarily provide when requesting registration as a participant or mentor, information, promotions, or GPUL services, as well as data later generated or exchanged to fulfil your request. We may also process data you provide through social networks to manage your requests, according to their privacy settings and policies.

### Participant and Mentor registration

- **Identification:** name, surname(s), identification document, city of residence, images or videos, and personal-website link.
- **Contact:** email address and telephone number.
- **Personal characteristics:** date of birth and T-shirt size.
- **Academic and professional:** CV, proof of student status, graduation year, university or school, current studies, and online professional profiles (GitHub, LinkedIn, Devpost).
- **Health:** allergies and food intolerances.

### Sponsor registration

- **Identification:** name, surname(s), and images or videos of the contact person or employee.
- **Business contact:** corporate email, company, and job title.
- **Health:** allergies and food intolerances.

## Purposes

For Participants and Mentors, GPUL manages requests, information, registration, promotions, and services before and during the event. For Sponsors, GPUL uses business contact data to maintain the commercial, contractual, or collaborative relationship and to provide services before and during the event.

GPUL may also send electronic commercial communications about similar activities, services, or products. Images and videos are processed for display, transmission, or publication with your explicit consent. Allergies and food intolerances are processed solely to manage catering.

## Legal basis

Processing Participant and Mentor data to manage requests is necessary for GPUL to fulfil its contractual obligations. Processing Sponsor business contact data and sending similar commercial communications are based on GPUL’s legitimate interest, as recognised by applicable law. You may object to commercial communications at any time by emailing [info@gpul.org](mailto:info@gpul.org).

Processing images, videos, allergies, and food intolerances is based on your explicit consent.

## Recipients and transfers

Data may be shared with public authorities, audit firms, and law-enforcement agencies where legally required; and with service providers that need access to provide services contracted by GPUL and have the required confidentiality and data-processing agreements.

At your request, we may share personal data in your CV with Sponsors of the event in which you participate, with your consent. We will inform you of future transfers to other recipients. GPUL has not contracted technology providers located in countries without data-protection rules equivalent to those of the European Union.

## Retention

We retain your data while your relationship with GPUL is active and, once it ends, for the legally applicable retention periods. Afterwards, data are retained only to demonstrate compliance with legal or contractual obligations, then deleted or anonymised.

## Your rights

You may access your data, rectify inaccurate data, request erasure when no longer needed, object to or restrict processing, and request portability using the postal and email addresses above. If you believe processing violates the law or your privacy rights, you may complain to GPUL or the Spanish Data Protection Agency through its postal or online channels.

## Changes and contact

This policy may change. We will notify you on this page and, for significant changes, through additional means including email. For questions, email [hackudc@gpul.org](mailto:hackudc@gpul.org).
